WHAT A TEAM GETS

Built for a team that trains together.

01

One team chat

Sessions, routines, and plans go in one chat. Choose whether everyone posts or only the staff, and athletes can still send the coaches a question.

02

A Team Calendar

Coaches plan a day or a repeating session for the whole team or a group. Each plan lands on every athlete's own calendar, with the coach's notes.

03

A board of who trained

A bar at the top of the team chat shows who has trained today, who leads on volume, and who has the longest streak.

04

Its own colors

Team colors and a banner teammates can hang on their profiles, and a team page anyone can open from it.

Joining is a link. Share it, or invite teammates straight from your contacts, then follow the whole team in one tap.

How does a team start? +

Anyone can make a team from Messages in the app and invite people with a link or from their contacts. Whoever makes it is asked whether they are the coach, and LOGR confirms each new coach before the coach tools switch on.

Who can change the Team Calendar? +

Only the coaching staff. Athletes see every plan that is for them on their own calendar, start it from there, and log it like any workout. A plan's notes go on the calendar, never into the chat.

Can athletes under 18 join a team? +

Yes. Teams work at every age from 13 up. Accounts under 18 cannot join group chats or communities, but they can join a team. Teen accounts has the rules.

What happens when someone leaves the team? +

The team's plans come off their calendar from that day on. Everything they already logged stays theirs, on their own account.

Does every athlete need an iPhone? +

Yes, for now. LOGR is an iPhone app, on the App Store in the United States and Canada.

Is LOGR free for teams? +

Yes. Everything on this page is free, with no ads and no subscription. Any future paid features will be clearly explained before they are introduced.
