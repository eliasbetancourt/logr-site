Log the work.

A training logbook that tracks every set, keeps your records straight, and lets you share a session with the people who train with you.

Get LOGR

Every set, not a summary

Reps, weight and rest per set. Timed holds count as time, never as reps. Start from a routine, or start with nothing and add lifts as you go.

Records that stay honest

Personal records are derived from your sessions, not typed in. Correct a weight you entered wrong and the record moves to whoever really earned it.

A streak that understands rest

Rest days count. So does training you did somewhere else. A streak should reflect a training week, not punish one.

Share the session, or don't

Post a workout with photos and notes, to everyone, to followers, or to nobody. Change your mind later, on any post.

A leaderboard that is just your people

Monthly standings across volume, workouts, sets, reps and streak, limited to the friends who follow you back. No global rankings.

No ads, no tracking

No analytics SDK, no advertising, no data sold. Your training is yours, and you can delete all of it from Settings.
