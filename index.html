HOW LOGR WORKS

Everything your training log should be.

Simple enough to use during every session. Powerful enough to become the record of everything you have accomplished.

01
LOG EVERY SESSION

Your entire workout, in one place.

Build routines, track sets and reps, record weight or time, and add notes without slowing down your session.

  • ✓ Log your own session, a team lift, or a coach's plan the same way
  • ✓ Create custom routines, or start with nothing and add as you go
  • ✓ Track lifts by weight and reps, holds by time
  • ✓ Sets and volume add up while you are still in the gym
  • ✓ See last time's numbers on every set
  • ✓ Name the session and write it up when you finish
02
SEE YOUR PROGRESS

Make the work visible.

Turn every logged session into a clear record of your consistency, your personal records, and your long-term progress.

  • ✓ Personal records worked out from your sessions, not typed in
  • ✓ Full history, and a calendar of every day you trained
  • ✓ Achievements, and a streak that counts rest days
  • ✓ A routine library you can reorder and reuse
  • ✓ A gallery of every photo you have posted
03

OUR STORY

We built the training app we wanted to use.

LOGR started with a simple frustration: training is social, but workout tracking is not.

As Division I athletes, we were surrounded by teammates working toward the same goals, yet everyone's routines, progress, and personal records lived in separate notes, spreadsheets, and apps.

We built LOGR to bring those pieces together. It is a place to log the work, see your progress, learn from the people around you, and stay accountable to the community that keeps you moving.

What is LOGR? +

LOGR is a social workout app for building routines, logging training, tracking progress, and sharing the work with friends and teammates. Whatever the session was, it goes in the same log.

Who is LOGR for? +

Athletes, gym-goers, teams, coaches, and anyone who wants a better way to record and share their training. You do not need a team or a coach to use it.

Can I use LOGR with my team? +

Yes. LOGR has group, team, and community chats today, so a squad can share sessions and routines in one place. A coach can run the team chat. Coach tools, like assigning routines, are part of LOGR's direction rather than something you can use right now.

I am just getting started. Is LOGR too much? +

No. There is nothing to set up and no plan to buy. Start a workout with nothing in it and add exercises as you go, or save a routine from a post and run that instead.

Is LOGR free? +

The initial version of LOGR is planned to be free. Any future paid features will be clearly explained before they are introduced.

When will LOGR be available? +

LOGR is currently preparing for release. Join the waitlist to receive launch and testing updates.

Can I post videos? +

Not yet. Today you can attach photos to a post, up to 20 of them. Video is something we want to add, and it is not in the app right now.

What if someone posts something offensive? +

Everything on LOGR is posted by other users and none of it is reviewed before it appears, so you may run into something explicit or offensive even though it is not allowed. You can report any message by pressing and holding it, and block any account from its profile. Messages are also filtered against a list of banned phrases before they send. We aim to look at every report within 24 hours. The community guidelines spell out the rules and what we do about them.

Does LOGR track me or sell my data? +

No. Worth separating two things that share a word: LOGR works out your volume, your records and your streak, and that analysis is the whole point of the app. What it does not do is measure you. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising, and no third-party trackers in the app, we do not record which screens you open, and we do not sell your data. You can delete your account and everything in it from Settings. See the Privacy Policy for the detail.
