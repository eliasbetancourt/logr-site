The workout log with your friends in it.

LOGR is a free workout log for iPhone. Log your lifts at the gym, post them, and see what your friends and teammates did today, with a new leaderboard every month.

On the App Store as LOGR - Train With Friends, in the US and Canada, with no ads. Built by Division I athletes.

Who LOGR is for.

For athletes

Keep every session, routine, PR, and milestone in one place, wherever the training came from.

For teams

Share the work, trade routines, and keep each other going in group and team chats.

For anyone starting out

No plan needed. Follow people whose training you like, save a routine from a post, and learn as you go.

For coaches and creators

Plan the team's training on a Team Calendar that lands on every athlete's own calendar, run the team chat, and share the routines you actually use.

More on LOGR for teams, coaches, college athletes, and training with friends.

Built by two D1 soccer players.

Elias Betancourt plays at Fordham and Cohen Williams plays at Temple. We lift with our teams every week, and everyone's numbers lived in their own notes app. So we built the log we wanted: quick between sets, with the people we train with in it.

FAQ

Still need help? Contact support →

Who is LOGR for? +

Athletes, gym-goers, teams, coaches, and anyone who wants a better way to record and share their training. You do not need a team or a coach to use it. You do need to be 13 or older.

Is LOGR free? +

Yes. LOGR is free, with no subscription, no ads, and none of your history behind a paywall.

Is LOGR on Android? +

No. LOGR is an iPhone app, for iOS 15.1 or later.

Is LOGR in my country? +

LOGR is on the App Store in the United States and Canada, and more countries are coming. If it is not available where you are, leave your email and we will tell you the moment it is.

We will only email you when LOGR is available in your region, and you can unsubscribe at any time. Your address is handled as described in our Privacy Policy.
Can I bring my workouts from another app? +

Yes. In Settings, Import Lifts reads a CSV file from another app or a spreadsheet and adds those lifts to your history. Export Lifts saves every lift as a CSV file.

Who can see my workouts? +

You choose who can see each post, and you can make your whole account private so only people you approve can follow you.

Is LOGR safe for teens? +

LOGR is for people 13 and older. Accounts under 18 keep Family-Friendly Language and photo blurring on and have limits on messages. Teen accounts has the details, including what parents can do.

What if someone posts something offensive? +

Slurs, harassment, and threats are censored before they are saved, and you can report anything or block anyone. Reports are reviewed daily and acted on within 24 hours. The community guidelines have the rules.

Does LOGR sell my data or show ads? +

No. There are no ads and no third-party trackers, and your data is never sold. To find what is broken or confusing, LOGR notes a few things about how the app is used, like which screens open, in its own database. Never anything you type. The Privacy Policy has the detail.
