WHAT FRIENDS DO HERE

Accountability, built in.

01

A feed of real workouts

Every post carries the session: volume, time, exercises, and up to 10 photos. Save any routine you see and run it yourself.

02

A monthly leaderboard

You and the people you follow, ranked by lifts, volume, longest streak, sets, reps, or rest days. A new board every month.

03

Streaks and pokes

Your streak counts every day you train or rest. Poke a friend to nudge them to work out today.

04

Train together

Tag the friends you lifted with: the session's photos run from one person to the next, with one like and one comment thread.

Share a workout or your streak as a story card to Instagram or TikTok, or send it to a group chat in the app.

Do my friends need LOGR? +

To show up in your feed and on your leaderboard, yes. Invite them from your contacts or send them your profile link.

Who sees my workouts? +

You choose who can see each post, and you can make your whole account private so only people you approve can follow you. Settings can also blur photos from people you do not follow.

What is a poke? +

A nudge. Your friend gets a notification that you nudged them to work out today. You can poke each friend once a day.

Is it only for lifting? +

No. Runs, classes, and any other activity go in the same log and count for your streak.

Is LOGR free? +

Yes. Everything on this page is free, with no ads and no subscription. Any future paid features will be clearly explained before they are introduced.
