Cookie policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

This website sets no cookies of its own, and runs no web analytics. That is why you were not shown a cookie banner: there was nothing to ask you about.

What that means in practice

Every page on this site is static HTML and CSS. There is no tag manager, no advertising pixel, no embedded video or social widget, and no third-party fonts loaded from someone else's server. Nothing here follows you to another site, and nothing here knows you were on one before.

One page runs one small script of our own. The home page shows a running total of how much has been lifted on LOGR, and a script we wrote and serve ourselves keeps that number up to date without you reloading the page. All it does is ask this site, every fifteen seconds, for those two public totals. It sets no cookie, it stores nothing in your browser, it sends nothing anywhere, and it never asks any server but this one. No other page on this site runs any script at all, and we serve no script written by anyone else.

The one exception, and it is not tracking

This site is hosted on Netlify. Like any web host, Netlify receives the requests your browser makes, which necessarily includes your IP address, and it may set a strictly necessary cookie to keep the service working, for example to protect against abuse. We do not control those, we do not read them, and they are not used to build a profile of you.

If you join the waitlist, the email address you type is sent to Netlify's form handling and stored for us there. That is a form submission rather than a cookie, and it is covered in the Privacy Policy.

The app

The LOGR iOS app is not a browser and does not use cookies. It keeps your sign-in token in your device's secure storage so you are not asked to log in every time, which is storage on your own phone and is not shared with anyone. What the app collects is set out in full in the Privacy Policy.

If this changes

If we ever add web analytics or anything else that stores data in your browser, this page will say so before it goes live, and we will ask for your consent where the law requires it.
