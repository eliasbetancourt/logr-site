LOGR vs Hevy

Checked November 7, 2026. Prices are from the US App Store that day.

LOGR and Hevy are both workout logs for lifting, with a feed of the people you follow. Hevy is the bigger app, on more devices, with a paid Pro plan. LOGR is for iPhone only, is free with no subscription, and has teams built in.

What Hevy does better

  • More devices. Android, Apple Watch, Wear OS, and the web. LOGR is on iPhone only and has no watch app yet.
  • A much bigger community. Hevy says more than 17 million athletes use it, so more of the people you know may already be there.
  • Guided programs. With Pro, Hevy Trainer builds a program and suggests the next weights and reps. LOGR does not.
  • Video, languages, and Strava. A workout can carry a video, the app is in 16 languages, and each workout can go straight to Strava.

Where LOGR is different

  • Nothing to pay for. No limit on routines or custom exercises, and all of your history.
  • Teams. A team page, chat, and Team Calendar, free for every athlete and coach on it.
  • A monthly race with friends. One leaderboard of the people you follow, new every month, plus streaks that count rest days, and pokes.
  • Training together. Tag who you lifted with, and the session's photos run from one person to the next, with one like and one comment thread. Up to 10 photos a post.

Which to pick

Hevy, if you use Android or a watch, want guided programs, or your friends already use it. LOGR, if you lift with friends or a team on iPhone and want all of it free. Trying LOGR loses nothing you have logged: your Hevy workouts come with you as a CSV file.

FAQ

Is LOGR free?

Yes. LOGR is free, with no subscription, no ads, and none of your history behind a paywall.

Can I move my workouts from Hevy to LOGR?

Yes. In Hevy, export your workouts as a CSV file from Profile, Settings, Export & Import Data. In LOGR, open Settings, tap Import Lifts, and pick the file. The lifts join your history with their dates, sets, reps and weights.

Is LOGR on Android?

No. LOGR is an iPhone app, for iOS 15.1 or later.

Can my team use LOGR?

Yes, for free. A team gets its own page and chat, and a confirmed coach plans training on a Team Calendar that lands on every athlete's own calendar. More on LOGR for teams.
