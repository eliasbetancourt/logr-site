Does my whole team need to be on LOGR? +

No. It works for one athlete logging their own lifts. It gets better as teammates join: one team chat, the coach's plans on everyone's calendar, and the daily board of who trained.

My coach doesn't use LOGR. Can I still log team lifts? +

Yes. Save the session as a routine and run it each time, or start an empty workout and add exercises as you go.

Can I log practice and conditioning? +

Yes. Anything that is not a lift goes in as an activity, with its time and, for a run or a ride, its distance, on the same calendar and streak.

What happens to my log when I leave a team? +

It stays with your account. Leaving a team takes its future plans off your calendar from that day on, and everything you already logged stays.

Is LOGR on Android? +

No. LOGR is an iPhone app, on the App Store in the United States and Canada.

Is LOGR free? +

Yes. Everything on this page is free, with no ads and no subscription. Any future paid features will be clearly explained before they are introduced.
