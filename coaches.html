WHAT A COACH CAN DO

Coach tools, in the app today.

01

Plan on a calendar

A single day or a repeating session, for the whole team or a group you saved. Athletes who join later get the whole-team plans too.

02

Attach the routine

Build the routine in LOGR, or pick one you already have. It opens for every athlete the plan is for, ready to start.

03

See who trained

Open any plan to see every athlete it is for and where each one stands: trained, still to do, or skipped.

04

Run the team chat

Post a plan to the chat as a card that changes when the plan does. Choose whether only staff post, and answer the questions athletes send.

Captains and Vice Captains help run the team. Notes you add to a plan go on athletes' calendars as yours, never into the chat.

Still need help? Contact support →

How do I set up as a coach? +

Make a team from Messages in the app and answer Yes when it asks whether you are the coach. LOGR confirms each new coach, and the coach tools switch on once it does. You can then make other coaches on the team.

Is LOGR free for coaches? +

Yes. Everything on this page is free, with no ads and no subscription. Any future paid features will be clearly explained before they are introduced.

Can athletes change what I planned? +

Each athlete gets their own copy on their own calendar and can skip a day, which the plan's list of athletes shows. Only the coaching staff can change the plan itself.

What can I see about my athletes? +

Who trained on a planned day, as far as each athlete's privacy settings allow. Everything else they log follows those same settings, like any account on LOGR.

Do my athletes need anything besides the app? +

No. They join the team from your link, and every plan for them shows up on their calendar. Teams work at every age from 13 up.
